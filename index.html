How a trade happens

Three parties, one line of English, and one click that only you can make.

  1. The agent proposes

    It reads the chart through Phosphor's tools and writes one line you can check against the screen: Long SOL $150 at 2x, market, stop 203.40, target 231.00.

  2. Phosphor checks

    The order is simulated on the venue and measured against your policy: size, the multiple, and how much collateral can be at risk in a day. A proposal outside the policy never reaches you.

  3. You release

    The proposal lands in the window. Yes is a button in the app, on a surface the agent cannot reach. Nothing moves until you click it.
