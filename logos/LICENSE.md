# Venue and token marks

- `hyperliquid.svg` and `hype.svg`: Hyperliquid's logo and mark (Hyperliquid_Light.svg and Hyperliquid_Blob_Green.svg from the brand kit at https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/brand-kit). Trademarks of Hyperliquid, used to identify the venue.
- `near.svg`: the NEAR wordmark as near.org serves it in its own header. A trademark of the NEAR Foundation, used to identify the venue.
- `eth.svg`: the branded Ethereum mark from `@web3icons/core` 4.0.56 (MIT), copied from the app's `ui/logos/`.
